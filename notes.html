Architecture Brief 01 • Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Cutting Enterprise Hallucinations by 67% with Hybrid Dense-Sparse-Graph RAG

The Failure Mode of Single-Vector RAG: Standard dense vector search (cosine similarity over chunked embeddings) degrades sharply on exact nomenclature (ticker symbols, gene identifiers, statutory citations) and fails completely when answering multi-hop synthesis queries that require traversing relational provenance across documents.

The Tri-Modal Architecture (AI Scholar): Across 15M+ scientific papers, I implemented a parallel three-stage retrieval and calibration pipeline: (1) Dense semantic recall via HNSW-indexed FAISS embeddings; (2) Exact lexical recall via BM25 with domain-tuned stemming; and (3) 2-hop citation & entity subgraph expansion in Neo4j constructed via spaCy and BERT-NER relation extraction. Candidates are merged via Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF) and routed by a MAML meta-learned adaptive retrieval policy (+40% NDCG@10).

Epistemic Abstention Guardrail: Rather than forcing generation when retrieval support is weak, the synthesis head runs Monte Carlo Dropout and Evidential Deep Learning over token-level Dirichlet concentration parameters. When epistemic uncertainty exceeds a calibrated threshold, the agent abstains or triggers a targeted follow-up query—cutting ungrounded claims by 67% on multi-hop synthesis benchmarks.

Architecture Brief 02 • Distributed Systems & Inference

Sub-100ms Domain LLM Inference via Custom CUDA Kernels & BPE Tokenizers

The Bottleneck: General-purpose tokenizers (e.g., standard LLaMA/tiktoken vocabularies) fragment domain-specific financial, quantitative, and scientific notation into 2.4× more subword tokens than necessary. Because Transformer self-attention scales quadratically with sequence length during prefill and linearly in KV-cache memory during decode, vocabulary fragmentation directly inflates both training cost and tail latency.

Custom Memory-Mapped CUDA Tokenization & ZeRO-3: For our 7B–14B parameter institutional foundation models at Braverock LLC, I trained domain-aligned BPE vocabularies and wrote zero-copy, memory-mapped C++/CUDA pre-tokenization pipelines paired with DeepSpeed ZeRO-3 stage-partitioned optimizer states.

Production Impact: Compressing effective sequence lengths by 29% and eliminating host-to-device serialization overhead reduced multi-GPU wall-clock step latency by 38% during training and enabled deterministic sub-100ms p99 inference across 10M+ indexed financial filings, earnings calls, and macro research reports.

  • DeepSpeed ZeRO-3
  • Custom CUDA Kernels
  • BPE Tokenizers
  • C++
  • vLLM
Architecture Brief 03 • High-Stakes AI & Differential Privacy

Epistemic Uncertainty Quantification as a Production Guardrail for Clinical & Enterprise AI

Why Softmax Probabilities Lie: Raw softmax logits measure relative class separation within the training distribution, not model familiarity. Out-of-distribution (OOD) inputs routinely produce 0.99+ softmax confidence, causing catastrophic silent failures in clinical diagnostics, autonomous trading execution, and enterprise compliance pipelines.

Decomposing Aleatoric vs. Epistemic Uncertainty: In both federated medical computer vision at RapidRads AI (EfficientNet-B7 across 4 hospitals under (ε = 1.2, δ = 10⁻⁵) differential privacy) and live RL trading agents at Braverock LLC, I separate irreducible data noise (aleatoric) from model ignorance (epistemic) using Evidential Deep Learning, Grad-CAM attribution, and stochastic weight/dropout ensembles.

Result: Achieving 0.972 ROC-AUC (96.5% sensitivity, 98.2% specificity) in cross-institutional clinical imaging while automatically routing high-epistemic-uncertainty cases to human clinicians, and maintaining a <1.9% maximum drawdown (6.2 Sharpe ratio) over 24 months of live quantitative execution.

  • Evidential Deep Learning
  • MC Dropout
  • Differential Privacy (ε-δ)
  • Federated Learning
