Engineering Notes & Architecture Briefs

Technical Deep Dives
Architecture Brief 01 • Retrieval Systems

Why Dense-Only Vector Search Fails at 15M+ Documents: Designing Tri-Modal Hybrid RAG

Standard dense-embedding RAG pipelines degrade sharply once a corpus crosses millions of technical documents. Dense bi-encoders compress whole passages into fixed-dimensional vectors, smoothing over exact mathematical notation, rare gene/chemical identifiers, and multi-hop citation structure.

In AI Scholar (15M+ scientific papers), we solved this by routing queries across three complementary indices: FAISS (semantic dense embeddings), BM25 (exact lexical and symbol matching), and Neo4j (citation and entity-relation knowledge graphs built via spaCy and BERT-NER). Using a Model-Agnostic Meta-Learning (MAML) retrieval router to dynamically weight each modality per query intent improved relevance by +40% NDCG@10 on our 200-query citation-grounded benchmark.

Architecture Brief 02 • Distributed Systems

Cutting 7B–14B LLM Step Latency by 38% with Custom CUDA Memory-Mapped Tokenizers & ZeRO-3

When pretraining and instruction fine-tuning domain-specific 7B–14B parameter foundation models across multi-GPU clusters, GPU compute often stalls on CPU-bound data loading, tokenization serialization, and all-gather communication overhead.

By pairing DeepSpeed ZeRO-3 parameter sharding with custom CUDA memory-mapped (`mmap`) pre-tokenized data streams and fused rotary-position kernels, we eliminated host-to-device serialization bottlenecks and overlapped communication with backward-pass computation—reducing wall-clock step latency by 38% across continuous training runs.

Architecture Brief 03 • High-Stakes AI Reliability

Calibrated Epistemic Uncertainty in Production LLMs & Federated Clinical Vision

In regulated domains—whether quantitative capital allocation, clinical diagnostics, or scientific synthesis—raw softmax probabilities are notoriously overconfident out-of-distribution.

Across both our federated clinical chest X-ray diagnostic system (0.972 ROC-AUC under ε = 1.2 differential privacy across 4 hospitals) and our chain-of-thought RAG synthesis pipelines, combining Monte Carlo dropout with evidential deep learning provides decomposed aleatoric and epistemic uncertainty bounds—allowing the system to abstain or flag low-confidence claims rather than hallucinate.
